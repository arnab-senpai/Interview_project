A balancer can work at two layers. At **layer 4** (TCP) it forwards whole connections without reading the request. At **layer 7** (HTTP) it ends the connection, reads each request, and can route by path, header or cookie. The difference matters most for long-lived connections: HTTP/2 and gRPC send many requests over one connection, so an L4 balancer that spreads connections can leave a few servers with most of the requests.
